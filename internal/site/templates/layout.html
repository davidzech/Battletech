Your seed is the key to your campaign progress. There is no login: anyone who has it can see and change your playthrough, so keep it somewhere safe. Enter the same seed on another device to continue there.

Name your company

Every company has a name in the lore, like Wolf’s Dragoons or the Kell Hounds. Yours is tracked under this seed, across every campaign it runs.

Players’ MekBay user IDs (needed to start)

Each player who plays a campaign needs their own MekBay user ID, so the force they take into a deploy is theirs in MekBay’s multiplayer lobby. Starting a campaign with N players needs Players 1 to N saved. An ID works like a password for that account’s MekBay forces, so only enter yours if you trust this site; it is never shown again. Your hangar and the OpFor are managed by the site and need none.
