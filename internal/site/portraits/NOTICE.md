# Pilot portraits

These portraits are artwork from **MechWarrior 5** (Piranha Games / Microsoft), collected by the project owner and
used here as named-pilot portraits. They are scaled to 192x192 and renamed by `gen_portraits.go`, otherwise
unchanged.
