# Keywords

The registry of every keyword a scenario can grant or require, in the front matter above. Keywords belong to the campaign as a whole, not to individual pilots, in the style of the campaign tracking in BattleTech: Aces (Scouring Sands).

Each keyword has an `id` (lowercase snake_case; scenarios refer to it by this only, and players never see it), the `name` players see, the one `scenario` that grants it (a scenario ID, which may be one not written yet), and a `description`, shown when players hover the keyword on the campaign overview (`""` until it is written).

This text is notes for authors and is not shown on the site.
