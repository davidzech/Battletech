# Scenario 006: Burning Chrome

## Briefing

Pup-class probes have found the Falcons' 7th Construction Binary on Evciler, raising a solar power array on the southern continent of Messian.

They mean to make the site a forward dropsite for the whole of the Peregrine Galaxy.

Follow the ice rift in. Leave them no power to land on.

## Overview

Pup-class probes have spotted the Jade Falcons' 7th Construction Binary setting up a solar power array on Messian, the southern continent of Evciler. Wolf command believes the Falcons intend to turn the area into a large forward dropsite for the entire Peregrine Galaxy. Navigate the narrow ice rift, destroy the solar array, and so prevent the enemy from setting up any major operation in the quadrant.
