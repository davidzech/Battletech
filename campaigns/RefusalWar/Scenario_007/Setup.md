# Scenario 007: Scorching Sand

## Briefing

Our 352nd Assault, the 3rd Battle Cluster and the 13th Wolf Guards have cut off the Falcons' 6th Provisional Garrison in the Great Gash. Their 5th Talon Cluster is hammering the 13th's flank to break the line.

Probes have found the 5th Talon's mobile headquarters, hidden among the spires and mesas of the Plain of Curtains. The Keshik wants it, and its staff, for interrogation.

Kill what guards it. The command vehicle itself must not be touched.

Let the Falcon remember Twycross.

## Overview

The Wolves' 352nd Assault Cluster, 3rd Battle Cluster and 13th Wolf Guards have cut off the Jade Falcons' 6th Provisional Garrison in the Great Gash, and the Falcons' 5th Talon Cluster is assaulting the 13th's flank to break the line. Probes have located the 5th Talon's mobile headquarters among the spires and mesas of the Plain of Curtains, and the Keshik has ordered the command vehicle and its staff captured for interrogation. Sweep the area to find the mobile HQ. Destroy any defenders, but leave the command vehicle unharmed. Once it is taken, set up a defensive position and hold it until your DropShip arrives.
