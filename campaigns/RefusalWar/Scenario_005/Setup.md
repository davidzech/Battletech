# Scenario 005: Sable Flame

## Briefing

The battle for Devin has begun. The Falcons' 2nd Jaegers and 1st Striker Clusters hold the surface.

Before our drop, we raised a sensor array in an abandoned base on Devin's moon, MacDuff. The Falcons have found it. They hold the landing pad, and they have taken its staff as bondsmen.

Kill every one of them. Not one shot may touch the dome: outside it there is no air.

Take it back, and hold it.

## Overview

The battle for the Devin system has begun, and the Jade Falcons' 2nd Jaegers and 1st Striker Clusters have dug in at strategic locations on the planet's surface. Ahead of the Wolf drop, a sensor array dome was set up covertly in an abandoned base on Devin's moon, MacDuff. Falcon command has learned where it is and landed a Fire Star at the site: it holds the landing pad and keeps the operations staff hostage as bondsmen. Destroy every defender to free the staff, without harming the dome: MacDuff has no breathable atmosphere, and a breach kills the personnel inside. Then hold the dome until more units arrive.

## Special Rules

**Gravity: 0.5 g.** Fall damage is multiplied by 0.5, rounded up. How low gravity changes jumping is TBD.

**Atmosphere: vacuum.** Extreme heat level 1: every unit gains 3 heat each turn.

**Forced withdrawal:** the OpFor is subject to forced withdrawal, off the east and south edges. If your campaign has the keyword **NO QUARTER**, it is not: instead, at the end of each phase, every OpFor unit that took damage this turn and is crippled rolls a piloting skill roll at +4, plus 1 for each pilot hit taken, or panics and ejects.

**Exits:** the players may withdraw off the west and north edges.

**Stray hits:** whenever a direct-fire weapon misses, roll 1d6.

- **1 to 3:** the shot hits an intervening hex in front of the target: 1, 2 or 3 hexes in front of it. If the attacker is 3 or fewer hexes from the target, it has no effect (the shot went into the sky).
- **4 to 6:** the shot hits the first hex behind the target whose level is higher than the target's. If there is none, it has no effect.

When indirect fire misses, roll its cluster hits as usual, then roll 1d6 for the hex around the target that takes the damage: 1 is north, counting clockwise (2 northeast, 3 southeast, and so on).

**The staff:** a staff member dies when the dome hex holding them is breached (its CF is gone). A staff member still alive when the last defender is destroyed is rescued.
