# Scenario 002: Flame Tongue

## Briefing

The 12th Falcon Regulars burned on Colmar. Their Khan has begun to sweat.

saKhan Phelan will not leave for the Inner Sphere before Delta Galaxy draws Falcon blood. On Sudeten, the 4th Wolf Guards have already broken the 8th Falcon Regulars. Their Star Colonel left his Dire Wolf in an ejection pod and went to ground in their headquarters. What remains of his command Star gathers there.

Leave them no one to command, and nothing to fly away on.

> I will skin the Wolves layer by layer until I have cut them to the bone.
>
> — Vandervahn Chistu, saKhan of Clan Jade Falcon

## Overview

To sell the bluff that every Wolf force is falling on the Falcons, saKhan Phelan and his Delta Galaxy must strike at least one world of the Jade Falcon Occupation Zone before they jump for the Inner Sphere. That world is Sudeten. An early ambush by the 4th Wolf Guards Cluster forced most of the 8th Falcon Regulars to withdraw, and their commander, Star Colonel Devin Buhallin, punched out of his Dire Wolf. Radar tracked his ejection pod to their headquarters, and the rest of his command Star is converging there for a last stand. Find and kill Star Colonel Buhallin in one of the hardened HQ buildings, kill Star Captain Rothgar Mattlov, who leads what is left of the command Star, and destroy the DropShip fuel pumping station so none of them can escape.

## Special Rules

**Reconnaissance:** before the battle, the players may reveal one objective of their choice, either by an orbital scan, which costs 100 SP from the warchest, or by dispatching a unit with the RCN special ability (Alpha Strike), which then takes no part in the battle.

**Forced withdrawal:** the player force is subject to forced withdrawal. The OpFor is too, except the Summoner.

**Exits:** the players may withdraw off the north, east or south edge. The scenario ends if every player unit has withdrawn.

**Time limit:** the scenario ends after 8 turns.

**Objectives:** a mech scans an objective when it ends its movement within 3 hexes of it (6 hexes for a mech with an active probe). A mech in range of several objectives picks one.

## Objectives

### Objective 1

Your scanners pick up the bio-signature of Star Colonel Devin Buhallin inside the building. Destroy the building, and the Star Colonel with it.

### Objective 2

Your scanners pick up a flood of electronic signals from the building. A moment later comes the troubling realization: a probe inside it has marked your mech.

In the combat phase of this turn, your mech is spotted by TAG and its TMM is reduced by 1.

### Objective 3

Your scanners identify a target of opportunity: a large cache of signals intelligence equipment is housed in this building. Destroy it.

### Objective 4

Your scanners find nothing.
