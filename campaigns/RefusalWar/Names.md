# Names

Example names for the Refusal War, offered as the placeholders of the force and pilot name boxes (the start wizard and the Recruit dialog). You play Clan Wolf during the Refusal War, so the force names are Wolf Clusters, Binaries, Trinaries and Stars in Clan style, some canon (the 13th Wolf Guards, the 341st Assault Cluster) and most made up in the same spirit; the pilot names are trueborn given names, without Bloodnames, since a fresh cadet has yet to win one.

Each list in the front matter replaces the site's general suggestions of the same kind; a list left out keeps them:

- `force_names`: force names, 1 to 40 characters.
- `male_pilot_names`, `female_pilot_names`: pilot names for the male and female portraits, 1 to 30 characters.
- `neutral_pilot_names`: names that suit any portrait; they are mixed into both lists above.
