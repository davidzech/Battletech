# Scenario 004: Temper Edge

## Briefing

The Falcons' 305th Assault Cluster has pushed deep into the forests of Findley Province, on Lowa. Our 341st Assault has met them.

During the enemy drop, a modified Mist Lynx, the C5, intercepted and decoded a Falcon transmission: their intended attack corridors. It went down in the snow of the Donner Rift.

A Savior repair vehicle is on its way to it. The machine is a convenience. The data core it carries is not.

Recover it at all costs.

## Overview

The Jade Falcons' 305th Assault Cluster has pushed far into the forests of Findley Province, on Baker 3's southern continent of Lowa, and the Wolves' 341st Assault Cluster has countered. During the enemy drop, a modified Mist Lynx reconnaissance variant intercepted and decoded a transmission listing the Falcons' intended attack corridors, and made it into the cover of the snow-blanketed Donner Rift before it went down. A Savior repair vehicle has been sent to field-repair the Mist Lynx for extraction, but the data core it carries comes first. A Falcon scout Star is already closing in. Take the data core from the downed mech and get it off the eastern edge, or repair the Mist Lynx and escort it out.

## Special Rules

**The data core:** a mech that stands still for a whole phase in a hex adjacent to the Mist Lynx copies its data core. Mark that mech with a token: it now carries a copy.

**Special orders:** every OpFor unit starts with Destroy Objective special orders, targeting the Savior once it reaches a hex adjacent to the Mist Lynx. The Mist Lynx is not a combat target while it is immobile (not yet repaired). Once it is repaired, every OpFor unit's Destroy Objective special orders target the Mist Lynx instead.

**The Savior repair vehicle:** its movement objective is a hex adjacent to the Mist Lynx, and it always moves along the road. It repairs the Mist Lynx by standing still in an adjacent hex, with its back turned to it, for 1 turn. Once the repair is done, it is subject to forced withdrawal.

**The Mist Lynx:** a fleeing scout. It starts with its pre-existing damage, immobile and prone. Once repaired it may try to stand; its movement objective is the easternmost paved road hex. Remember to apply its quirks.

**Automata:** the players' automata always move first, the Savior and then the Mist Lynx. They need no Aces deck: the special order card is enough.

**Protector:** every pilot of a heavy or assault mech gains the Protector Edge ability for this mission. A pilot who already has it gains 1 extra Edge token instead.

**Forced withdrawal:** the OpFor is subject to forced withdrawal.

**Exits:** a mech carrying a copy of the data core, and the repaired Mist Lynx, escape off the eastern edge.

**The scenario ends** when every copy of the data core has been destroyed (the Mist Lynx and any mech carrying a copy), or when every unit of one side has been destroyed.

**Objectives:** shuffle Objective 1 and Objective 2 and place both, face down, on turn 5. If your campaign has the keyword **SIGINT**, move the top unrevealed one to turn 3.
