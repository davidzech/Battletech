# Scenario 003: Blade Splint

## Briefing

The Falcons' 10th Provisional Garrison saw us coming. 

Their mobile headquarters is gone from where it stood, moved somewhere within Salyn, near the Nalon Spaceport.

A hidden headquarters still needs power. Follow the NAV sequence to the city's plasma converter, and learn what it burns.

> The Crusaders may be your philosophical kin, but they are not Wolves. You hate the Inner Sphere because of our teachings. We are told that they are below us, yet on every front they have adapted and are now a match for most of the Clans. Perhaps before we assault them again, we should study their ways of war, lest we run the risk of being defeated just as the Falcons, your philosophical leaders, were.
>
> — Star Colonel Ulric Kerensky, to the 11th Wolf Regulars before the drop to Zoetermeer

## Overview

Intelligence suspects that the Jade Falcons' 10th Provisional Garrison, anticipating the Wolf assault on Zoetermeer, has moved its headquarters to a hidden site within the city of Salyn, near the Nalon Spaceport. If it has, the city's plasma power converter will most likely have been modified to handle karyoplasma. Enter the area, follow the NAV sequence through the structures and find the power converter, recognizable by its bright plasma flow. Inspect it to see whether it has been modified for karyoplasma conversion. If it has, destroy it. If it hasn't, destroy the garrison's force commander and scan their mech for intelligence on the hidden headquarters.
