## Briefing

## The Enemy Within

*June 21, 3057\
Five years after the Battle of Tukayyid*

Repeated calls for a repudiation of the Truce of Tukkayid has resulted in growing tensions between the Crusader and Warden factions of the Clans. Clan Wolf’s success on Tukayyid led to internal strife as young Crusader warriors, eager for advancement, resented the peace and the dominance of older Warden warriors.

Crusader elements within the Clan called Wolf leadership to trial, leading many to an early death by the hand of **Khan Natasha Kerensky**.

Crusader Loremaster Dalk Carns, along with Star Captain Vladimir Wolf, embittered by their poor results on Tukkayid, have levied serious accusations against Ulric, hoping to depose him an effort to catch up with Clan Wolf in the race for Terra. Accused of conspiring with ComStar in destroying a Clan’s genetic heritage, Ulric, anticipating the Crusaders’ intentions, strategically redirected the charges to the Grand Council with the Jade Falcons as the primary prosecutor, where he was narrowly convicted. 

In a stroke of genius, Ulric rejected his fate, initiating a Trial of Refusal against his verdict. Seeking to preserve the honor of the Warden way, Urlic dispatched saKhan Phellan (Kell) Ward and a full Galaxy of Warden aligned Wolves to the Inner Sphere to establish a safe haven, leaving the remaining Crusader Wolves to fight Jade Falcon in what amounts to a Trial of Annihilation in all but name.
