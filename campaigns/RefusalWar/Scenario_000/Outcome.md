# Scenario 000: Trial of Position - Outcome

## Victory & Rewards

Each named pilot earns the following SP for the number of kills for this scenario.

| Kills | SP  |
|-------|-----|
| 1     | 150 |
| 2     | 400 |
| 3     | 650 |
| 4     | 900 |

If your pilot dies, or fails to kill at least 1 opponent, they are permanently removed from the campaign. You will receive a new named pilot with 0 starting SP for Sortie 001.
